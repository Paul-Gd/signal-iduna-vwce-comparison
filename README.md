# SIGNAL IDUNA "101 Invest" vs. DIY VWCE — Independent Analysis

A data-driven comparison of an **OVB Allfinanz (Romania)** investment proposal — redirecting half of a monthly index-fund contribution into a **SIGNAL IDUNA *101 Invest*** unit-linked life-insurance product — against simply continuing to hold a low-cost global index ETF (**VWCE**, Vanguard FTSE All-World) in a brokerage account.

## TL;DR

**The insurance product loses on every leg that matters — risk-adjusted performance, all-in cost, and after-tax outcome.** Recommendation: keep the low-cost index ETF.

- Over Jul 2020–May 2026, **VWCE beat all 8 funds on the product's menu** on return, drawdown, and Sharpe — strict dominance, even before the insurance wrapper's fees are subtracted.
- The proposal headlines a "0.90% all-in cost" but **omits the underlying active funds' TER** (~1.5–2%), making realistic drag ~2.4%/yr vs the ETF's 0.19%.
- The product's only structural edge — deferred tax — is a **one-time 16% on exit**, far smaller than a **recurring ~2.4%/yr** compounding cost. A corrected model has the ETF ahead at every horizon, and the gap widens over time.
